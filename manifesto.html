The Covenant

Five commitments, held in public.

We build AI for communities that are — with good reason — hostile to AI. That work isn't earned with a values page. It's earned with commitments specific enough to be broken, published where breaking them would be unmissable. Here they are.

§ 02WHY WRITE IT DOWN

Because the incentive to break it will arrive on schedule.

Some quarter in 2029, selling readings directly will look like easy revenue. Some acquirer in 2031 will want the pooled voice models. The Covenant exists for those meetings — a pre-commitment made when thinking was clear, priced into every contract our practitioners sign. AstroTalk's collapsed $100M deal — flagged in diligence over user-generated content — is the cautionary tale: in this category, what you tolerate is what you're worth.
