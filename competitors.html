The landscape

A field split in two — and the middle left open.

Hundreds of millions in venture capital has gone into this category and none of it equips a practitioner. Consumer apps monetize attention; APIs monetize computation. The seat between them — Shopify + voice model + compliance + attestation — is empty. We checked. Twice.

The threat we respect

The horizontal bolt-on.

Circle or Patreon could ship a “spiritual template” in a quarter. What they won't ship: a licensed ephemeris core, cited tradition corpora, verified-human attestation, high-risk underwriting, or refusal guardrails with statutory teeth. Our defense is depth — and if a horizontal or an API vendor moves anyway, the plan says it plainly: accelerate Synthesis and the community moat, or pursue acquisition from strength.

The blueprint we follow

SimplePractice, translated.

One founder's insight — solo professionals need vertical software plus payments — grew to 225,000+ clinicians inside a ~$4B deal. Therapists then; practitioners now. Same fragmentation, same solo-professional economics, same wedge — with one addition the 2010s never needed: proof of the human.
