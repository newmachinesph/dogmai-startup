No — structurally no. We're B2B2C: tools to practitioners, never readings to seekers. The AI drafts; you sign; Attest proves the human. Breaking that would destroy our moat, not just our manners. It's commitment №1 of the Covenant.

Then don't use it. Storefronts, payments, booking, memberships, and Attest all work with fully-hand-made readings — the seal simply records zero AI steps, which for many readers is the whole point.
