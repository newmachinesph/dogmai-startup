§ 01THE LOOP

Draft → refine → sign

The order is the ethic. AI touches the reading first; the human touches it last — always, structurally, and on the record.

☉

The chart is computed

Engine casts the chart deterministically — licensed ephemeris math, never language-model arithmetic. Corpus assembles the tradition-grounded raw material, citations attached.

☿

Voice drafts, in yours

Your model composes the way you would — your metaphors, your pacing, the topics you refuse. First drafts read like you on a good day, not like a horoscope mill.

✳

You sign, Attest seals

You edit, approve, and sign. The seal records human review and discloses the AI-assisted steps. Skip the AI entirely and the seal says that, too.

Corpus

Grounded in lineage, or it doesn't ship.

Generic AI invents cosmology because nothing anchors it. Corpus anchors every claim to named, versioned tradition corpora — so your drafts cite Hellenistic technique or the Rider-Waite-Smith lineage, not the model's vibes.

  • Named traditions — Hellenistic & psychological astrology, jyotiṣa, esoteric tarot, Pythagorean numerology at launch
  • Versioned & auditable — every draft can show its sources on request
  • Your tradition, your rules — exclude schools you don't practice; add your own teaching materials

What trains what

SPEC. 02
Trains your Voice: readings you upload, notes you write, edits you make to drafts.
Grounds it: the cited tradition corpora you enable.
Never trains anything: your clients' readings, seeker memory, other practitioners' models.

Ownership is contractual: on departure, your model exports with you and our copy is deleted.

§ 02WHY THIS SIDE OF THE LINE

The commoditization curve has a right side.

Translation lost over a third of its working base to generative AI. Stock photographers now compete with their own training data. In every case the surviving value pooled in the same place: relationship, brand, and provenance. Voice + Attest is that place, productized for this trade — the practitioner-empowering answer in a community that is rightly hostile to replacement AI.
