“A human read this.” Now prove it.

AI made generic interpretation free and infinite. Three legislatures made the human/AI boundary a legal requirement. Attest is where those two facts become your advantage: a cryptographic seal on every reading — verifiable by anyone, dependent on no one, including us.

§ 01THE THREE CLAIMS

What the seal binds to a reading

Ⅰ

Authorship

A verified human practitioner authored this reading — or reviewed and signed an AI-assisted draft. Verification means identity-checked onboarding, not a checkbox.

§ 02WHY NOW

Regulation converged on exactly this boundary

Disclosure-when-AI is no longer an ethic — it's a statute with a private right of action. The full legal landscape lives on the Guardrails page; these are the three that make Attest a compliance feature.

California SB 243

Effective Jan 1, 2026. Clear and conspicuous notice when a reasonable person could think the bot is human. Self-harm protocols. Private right of action — plaintiffs' lawyers included.

New York GBL §1700

AI Companion Models statute, effective Nov 5, 2025. Disclosure and safety duties for AI companions; Utah and Maine moved the same year. Ten-plus states expected by 2030.

EU AI Act · Art. 5

In force since Feb 2025, fines to €35M or 7% of turnover. Prohibits AI that exploits vulnerability into psychological or financial harm — with wellness chatbots cited in Commission guidance.

The pattern across all three: the law rewards whoever can prove the human. That's not a burden on this trade — it's a gift to it.

Precedent

“Human-made” already commands the premium.

CHANI runs an explicit zero-AI, all-human-written editorial policy — no ads, no VC — at an estimated ~$14M a year with a million monthly readers. The willingness to pay for verified human craft isn't our hypothesis. It's someone else's P&L.

  • Provenance is the retained value — translation and stock photography show generic work commoditizing while attributed work holds
  • C2PA (Adobe, BBC, Microsoft, Sony) built the rails but no free path for individuals — Attest is that path for this vertical
  • We validate the premium empirically with the founding cohort, rather than assuming it
