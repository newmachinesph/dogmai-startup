§ 02THE RISKS

What kills this company, ranked

From our own market map, unsoftened. Each pairs with its mitigation — and the honest ones double as the moat.

1 · Payments
Highest-probability killer: processors restrict the category, and aggregating hundreds of such merchants concentrates the risk. Mitigation: MoR/high-risk relationships secured before scaling; honest category coding; guardrails as underwriting evidence. Published pivot: software-only atop practitioners' approved processors if unsolved by 2028.
2 · App stores
Apple's 4.3 rejects lookalike fortune-telling apps serially; a white-label fleet is a mass-rejection machine. Mitigation: web-first/PWA distribution, differentiated branding, IAP-compliant checkout where unavoidable.
3 · AI regulation
EU AI Act Article 5, SB 243's private right of action, FTC enforcement. Mitigation: this risk is the product — guardrails, attestation, truthful claims. Every tightening buries a competitor.
4 · Community backlash
Practitioner communities are openly hostile to AI; one misstep reads as betrayal. Mitigation: practitioner-owned augmentation, human attestation, the Covenant's no-consumer-readings line held absolutely.
5 · Solo-founder bandwidth
Eight pillars on weekends will not outpace a funded entrant on full scope. Mitigation: buy the ephemeris, sequence ruthlessly, and raise by ~2028 or reach durable profitability — the map's own condition.
