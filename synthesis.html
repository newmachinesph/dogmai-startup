Memory

Met as a person, not an intake form.

A returning client shouldn't have to re-explain the career question from March or the grief from last winter. Memory holds the longitudinal thread — past readings, stated intentions, what mattered — so session five builds on session four. It's also the quiet retention engine: context this deep is why a practice on DOGM.AI is genuinely hard to leave, and why we make leaving genuinely easy. The lock-in must be value, never hostage-taking.
