§ 01THE HOSTILE TERRAIN

Named a restricted business by every default rail

Processors

Stripe lists “psychic services and fortune tellers” as restricted — prior written approval required, banned outright in some jurisdictions. Practitioners petition against cancellations triggered by the words on their checkout page.

App stores

Apple's Guideline 4.3 groups fortune-telling with spam; astrology apps report serial rejections — Struck, roughly ten times. Aggregating hundreds of lookalike practitioner apps would concentrate that risk. So we don't.

Ad platforms

Meta's Personal Attributes policy bars ads implying a viewer's religion or emotional state — enforcement increasingly automated. “You”-style spiritual copy dies in review. Compliant templates ship with every storefront.

The answer

Aggregate the risk, then underwrite it properly.

One practitioner is a rounding error to a processor. A network of attested, guardrailed, honestly-coded practices is an underwritable book of business. That's the deal Commerce makes on your behalf.

  • High-risk / merchant-of-record relationships negotiated for spiritual services — secured before scaling, per the plan
  • Honest merchant-category coding — no euphemisms that unravel at the first audit
  • Conservative refund & chargeback controls — the numbers underwriters actually read
  • The Guardrails audit trail as evidence — compliance doing double duty in underwriting
