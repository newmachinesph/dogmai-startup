About

A company built the way a chart is cast: from first principles, at a known hour.

DOGM.AI started with a question sharp enough to fail: can one founder build the category leader in practitioner infrastructure by 2033? We commissioned the research, published it — flat demand curve, hostile processors, skeptical communities and all — and then started building exactly what the map said to build.

§ 01THE PREMISE

Two truths, one company

First: generic AI interpretation is commoditizing fast — what happened to translation and stock photography is arriving for readings. Second: the value that survives commoditization pools in relationship, brand, and provenance — the things practitioners have and platforms don't. A company that equips the practitioner side of that asymmetry — payments, voice, proof — is the one the moment actually calls for. So we're building it, in the open, on a seven-year clock.

Founded
2026 — nights-and-weekends validation, by design. The map's Phase I calls for 25 hand-recruited practitioners before anything scales.
Model
Vertical SaaS + payments for solo professionals — the SimplePractice shape, applied to a $2.3B fragmented trade the processors treat as contraband.
Discipline
B2B2C, permanently. We sell tools to practitioners and never readings to seekers — the commitment the whole moat hangs on. The Covenant holds the rest.
Team
Deliberately near-solo through 2027: a founder, a founding engineer, a practitioner community lead, fractional counsel. Small enough to be honest, senior enough to ship. Three seats open.
Funding
Bootstrapped through validation. The map's gate is explicit: at $100K MRR, choose deliberately between a seed round and staying lean — both are winning paths; drifting into either isn't.
The name
Dogma is a received truth. DOGM.AI is the received truth infrastructure — the unglamorous certainty beneath practices that trade in mystery: payments that clear, seals that verify, guardrails that hold.
